    <p class="home-section__lead">

    </p>

<section class="home-section">
  <p class="home-section__number">02</p>
  <div>
    <h2>What We Do</h2>
    <p>
      We study embodied and adaptive intelligence across robot morphology, sensing, nonlinear dynamics, modeling, system identification, control, neuromorphic computation, online adaptation, and lifelong learning.
    </p>

    <div class="home-pillar-grid">
      <article class="home-pillar">
        <h3>Embodied & Bio-Inspired Robots</h3>
        <p>Design dynamic robotic bodies whose morphology, mechanics, and sensing contribute directly to behavior.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="home-pillar">
        <h3>Dynamics & System Identification</h3>
        <p>Understand nonlinear, coupled, time-varying, and underactuated physical systems through modeling and data.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="home-pillar">
        <h3>Adaptive & Learning-Based Control</h3>
        <p>Develop controllers that combine physical models with learning and adaptation.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="home-pillar">
        <h3>Neuromorphic & Lifelong Intelligence</h3>
        <p>Explore efficient neural computation and continual adaptation for robots operating under real-world constraints.</p>
      </article>
    </div>

    <p class="home-platform-note">
      Flapping-wing robots are one of our flagship testbeds. Their aerodynamics, compliant structures, sensing, actuation, and control are inseparably coupled, making them ideal systems for studying embodied intelligence under real physical constraints.
    </p>

    <p class="home-inline-link"><a href="/research/">Explore Our Research &rarr;</a></p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <p class="home-section__number">03</p>
  <div>
    <h2>How We Get There</h2>
    <p>
      We do not treat intelligence as software added after the robot is built. We co-design the body and the brain.
    </p>

    <div class="home-pipeline" aria-label="Build, understand, learn, adapt research pipeline">
      <article>
        <span>Build</span>
        <p>Create robotic bodies whose morphology, mechanics, actuation, and sensing are designed together.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>Understand</span>
        <p>Model and identify the nonlinear physical dynamics governing their behavior.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>Learn</span>
        <p>Use data-driven, neural, and neuromorphic methods to extract useful structure from interaction.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>Adapt</span>
        <p>Close the loop on physical robots so behavior can change with uncertainty, new environments, and accumulated experience.</p>
      </article>
    </div>

    <p class="home-method-line">
      From mechanics to mathematics, and from neural computation to real-world experiments, NEAR works across the complete loop of embodied intelligence.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <p class="home-section__number">04</p>
  <div>
    <h2>What We Value</h2>
    <div class="home-principle-grid">
      <article>
        <h3>Intelligence is embodied.</h3>
        <p>The body is not merely a plant to be controlled. Morphology and dynamics can shape sensing, computation, and behavior.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Learning meets physics.</h3>
        <p>We combine data-driven intelligence with physical models, dynamics, and prior structure rather than treating robots as black boxes.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Robots should keep adapting.</h3>
        <p>Real environments change. Robots should improve and adapt beyond their original training conditions.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Ideas must survive reality.</h3>
        <p>Simulation is valuable, but physical experiments are the ultimate test.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Breakthroughs cross boundaries.</h3>

<section class="home-section home-section--future">
  <p class="home-section__number">05</p>
  <div>
    <h2>Toward machines that adapt as naturally as living systems.</h2>
    <p>
      Our long-term goal is to create robots that can sense through their bodies, exploit their own dynamics, learn from experience, and continually adapt to the world around them while remaining efficient, interpretable, and trustworthy.
    </p>
    <p class="home-manifesto">
      Intelligence should not be confined to an algorithm running inside a machine. It should emerge from the machine as a whole.
    </p>
    <p class="home-signoff">NEAR the future of embodied intelligence.</p>
